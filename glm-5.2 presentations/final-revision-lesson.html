Practice activity · Describe & build

Inside the classroom

Build a six-sentence report

  1. Use there is / there are.
  2. Describe two actions with Present Continuous.
  3. Make one comparison.
  4. Guess what happened before with Past Simple.
  5. Add an experience with Present Perfect.
  6. End with a First Conditional.
There are several students around a table. They are comparing their notes and one student is explaining an idea. The group on the right looks busier than the others. They started this task earlier. They have already written some answers. If they finish soon, they will present their work.

Speaking review · 6 minutes

The six-question interview

  1. What do you usually do after school?
  2. What are you working on right now?
  3. What did you do last weekend?
  4. Have you ever learned something difficult?
  5. What will you do next month?
  6. If you have more free time, what will you change?
Follow-up bank: Why? · How often? · What happened next? · How did you feel? · Would you do it again?
