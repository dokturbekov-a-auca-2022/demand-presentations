Grammar station 1

Routine or right now?

Present Simple

S + base verb(s)

Use: habits, facts, schedules

Signals: usually, every day, often

She plays volleyball on Fridays.

− She doesn’t play…
? Does she play…?

Present Continuous

S + am/is/are + V-ing

Use: now, temporary situations

Signals: now, at the moment, today

She is playing online now.

− She isn’t playing…
? Is she playing…?

Common mistake: “I am knowing him.” → “I know him.”
Controlled practice

Choose the camera mode

1. I usually ___ online classes from my room.

2. Look! It ___ outside.

3. My brother ___ video games every evening.

4. This week, we ___ with our grandparents.

Grammar station 2

Finished past or connected to now?

Past Simple

S + V2 / did + base

Finished action + finished time

We went skiing last January.

− We didn’t go…
? Did you go…?

Present Perfect

S + have/has + V3

Experience / result now / unfinished time

I have tried snowboarding.

Signals: ever, never, just, already, yet, recently

Common mistake: “I have visited Sam yesterday.” → “I visited Sam yesterday.”
Controlled practice · Gap fill

Past or perfect lab

1. I that film last winter. (see)

2. She ice skating. (never / try)

3. We our homework. (just / finish)

4. They to the coast in July. (travel)

Grammar station 3

Future ideas & real results

Future Simple: will

S + will + base verb

prediction · quick decision · promise · offer

I think it will snow tonight.

− It won’t snow.
? Will it snow?

First Conditional

If + Present, will + base

possible future condition + result

If it snows, we will build a snowman.

Result can come first: We’ll stay home if it rains.

Common mistake: “If it will rain…” → “If it rains…”
Speaking game · Whole class

Grammar station 4

Build a scene with there is / are

Singular / uncountable

There is + a/some…
There is a lake near the camp.

− There isn’t a lake.
? Is there a lake?

Plural

There are + plural noun
There are three cabins.

− There aren’t any cabins.
? Are there any cabins?

Common mistake: “There have many people.” → “There are many people.”
Grammar station 5

Turn differences into comparisons

Short adjectives

adj + -er + than

cold → colder
hot → hotter

Winter is colder than autumn.

Long adjectives

more + adj + than

interesting → more interesting

Travel is more exciting than homework.

Irregular

good → better

bad → worse
far → farther/further

This year is better than last year.

Reading · From posts to grammar

Two updates, two seasons

Leo · Summer

I usually spend summer in the city, but last July my family travelled to a small coastal town. There were colourful cafés near the beach, and the sea was warmer than I expected. I’ve never learned to surf, so I took three lessons. I fell a lot, but it was the highlight of my trip! If we return next year, I’ll practise again.

Nora · Winter

Winters are normally quiet for me. This month, however, I’m staying with my cousin in the mountains. It has snowed twice this week, and everything looks brighter than at home. Yesterday we built a tiny snow fort. I think it will disappear soon if the temperature rises.
