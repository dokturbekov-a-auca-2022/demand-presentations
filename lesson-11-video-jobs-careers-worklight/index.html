Workplaces

Tap a workplace. Notice: one job can have several workplaces.
5 min. Tap each stage. Students choose the correct preposition: in a studio, on a rooftop, at a concert.

Career skills

5 min. There is no single correct brightest skill. Students justify choices using because.

Listening interview

"I listen for what other people miss."

First listen: What does Nara enjoy? Second listen: Which skills does she mention?

I work in a small recording studio. I have to prepare the microphones before each session. During a recording, I listen carefully and solve sound problems. I need to communicate with singers because they cannot always hear what I hear. I enjoy the moment when many small choices become one clear song.
6 min. Play the interview twice while the transcript stays hidden. First: general meaning. Second: students note workplace, duties, skills and what Nara enjoys.
