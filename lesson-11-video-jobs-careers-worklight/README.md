# Lesson 11 - WORKLIGHT

An offline, teacher-led Pre-Intermediate 2 video lesson for learners aged 13-17.

- Topic: Jobs and Careers
- Lesson type: original visual documentary with teacher-read narration
- Creative identity: a cinematic black-box studio where every career appears under a different work light
- Scene count: 27
- Main file: `index.html`

## Teaching Route

The lesson covers job and workplace vocabulary, responsibilities, career skills, pronunciation, pre-viewing prediction, a playable four-frame visual documentary, two viewing passes, comprehension, sequencing, useful career language, controlled practice, a teacher-read interview, information gap, timed production, review, homework and exit reflection.

## Controls

- `Right Arrow`, `Page Down`, or `Space`: next scene
- `Left Arrow` or `Page Up`: previous scene
- `M`: scene map
- `N`: teacher notes
- `F` button: fullscreen
- Swipe left or right on touch devices

All runtime assets are local. No network connection is required.
