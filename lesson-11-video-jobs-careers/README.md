# Lesson 11 — Career Quest

An offline, interactive 22-scene video lesson for Pre-Intermediate 2 learners (ages 13–17): **Jobs & Careers**.

The video component is an original visual storyboard with teacher-read narration, so it works reliably without streaming or external links.

## Controls

- Arrow keys, Page Up/Page Down, Space, or swipe: navigate
- M: directory
- N: teacher notes
- F: browser fullscreen

## Major activities

- Job/workplace matching and skills language
- Two-viewing visual-storyboard tasks
- Detail hunt and comprehension check
- Secret-career information gap
- Timed career interview and 45-second career-card production
- Review quiz, homework, and exit ticket

All answers can be fictional. Students do not need to share personal family or career information.
