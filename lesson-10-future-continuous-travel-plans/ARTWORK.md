# Artwork prompts — Future Postcards

All three illustrations were generated specifically for this lesson with the
built-in image-generation workflow.

## future-postcards-hero.png

Whimsical travel scrapbook scene with a teenage traveler looking through a
giant cardboard camera window into several future travel moments: airport
departure lounge, sleeper train, beach ferry, and mountain cable car. Gouache,
colored pencil, cut-paper edges, paper texture, open space on the left for
title text, no readable words or logos.

## future-trip-story.png

Playful airport-to-train travel itinerary with three teenagers and a guide
moving through future travel moments: departure board, train, ferry sunrise,
and mountain hostel. Scrapbook spread, dotted route, ticket corners, gouache
and colored pencil, four connected snapshot panels, no readable text.

## future-window-speaking.png

Whimsical future-window scene inside a tiny treehouse observatory. Teenagers
look through colorful round windows at a camper van, hot-air balloon, snowy
train, and boat under stars. Paper collage, star stickers, compass doodles,
warm yellow windows, no readable text or logos.
