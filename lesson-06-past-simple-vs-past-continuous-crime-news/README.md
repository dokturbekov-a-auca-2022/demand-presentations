# Lesson 06 — Breaking at 9:17

Interactive 26-scene HTML lesson for **Past Simple vs Past Continuous | Crime
& News Stories** (Pre-Intermediate 2, ages 13–17).

Open `index.html` in a current browser and keep the `assets` folder beside it.
The lesson has no external runtime dependencies.

## Controls

- Arrow keys, Page Up/Page Down, Space, or swipe: change scene
- `M`: newsroom rundown
- `N`: teacher notes
- `F`: fullscreen or iPhone display instructions

## Visual direction

The presentation is a neon-noir night newsroom: cinematic cel-shaded artwork,
scan lines, live tickers, ultraviolet shadows, cyan data panels, orange alerts,
and a vertical broadcast control rail. It deliberately avoids the museum/guild,
analogue-radio, stop-motion, and risograph-collage identities of earlier decks.

## Lesson route

Students examine a fictional, non-violent missing-trophy mystery. They learn
crime/news vocabulary and collocations, discover the tense contrast, build
forms, scrub a CCTV timeline, practice when/while, complete controlled tasks,
read a careful first report, classify verb jobs, interview witnesses,
reconstruct the solution, compare headlines, and produce a corrected news
bulletin.

The apparent crime has an innocent solution: a cleaner moved the cup away from
rainwater during a blackout. This supports both grammar practice and responsible
reporting without accusing or frightening students.

Exact prompts for the four original illustrations are in `ARTWORK.md`.
