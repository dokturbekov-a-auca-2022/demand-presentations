# Original artwork prompts

All four images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool and
saved locally as compressed WebP assets. No activity text is baked into them.

```text

Subject: four clearly separated people caught in different ongoing actions: a young event photographer turning toward the blackout, a middle-aged security guard checking a side door, a teenage athlete carrying folded chairs, and a cleaner moving quickly near a storage-room entrance; expressions surprised, no one threatening
Style/medium: premium neon-noir graphic novel illustration, crisp cel shading, sharp cinematic diagonals, luminous rim light, deep shadows, smooth ink shapes, no collage, no risograph, no clay, no stop-motion
Composition/framing: widescreen cinematic establishing shot, empty pedestal as the visual focal point, readable actions for classroom prediction, generous dark negative space for HTML title overlay
Lighting/mood: tense and mysterious but safe, like a clever family mystery rather than violent crime
Color palette: midnight navy, ultraviolet purple, electric cyan, warning orange, acid lime highlights
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, weapons, violence, police tape, injury, fear, watermark, distorted hands, duplicate people
```
