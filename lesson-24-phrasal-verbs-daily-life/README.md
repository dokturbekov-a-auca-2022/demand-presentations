# Lesson 24 - Everyday Action Studio

An offline-first, teacher-led Pre-Intermediate 2 lesson on phrasal verbs in daily life for students aged 13-17.

The 28-scene deck deliberately abandons the series' earlier card-and-panel language. Its visual identity is kinetic editorial collage: oversized type, torn-paper photography, transparent objects, changing particle words, diagonal composition, and five camera-like scene transitions.

The learning journey follows one day from waking up to returning home. Students discover particle meaning, practise separable verbs and pronoun position, complete controlled tasks, listen to a voice note, solve everyday problems, read for evidence, perform online charades, build a seven-person story, and deliver a 60-second final performance.

Controls: arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Space, stable one-finger horizontal swipe, `M` for the cut list, and `N` for teacher notes. Vertical slide scrolling and pinch zoom remain available on phones.

All artwork and audio are local. No external runtime is required.
