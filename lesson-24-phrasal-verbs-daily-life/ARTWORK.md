# Artwork

Nine original raster assets were generated for this lesson with the built-in ImageGen tool and saved locally in `assets/`.

- `action-studio-hero-v2.png` - full-bleed opening collage
- `morning-triptych-v2.png` - wake up, put on, and pick up sequence
- `kitchen-chaos-v2.png` - breakfast problem scene
- `city-motion-v2.png` - public transport and meeting actions
- `lost-keys-v2.png` - visual search scene
- `seven-actions-v2.png` - exactly seven online action performers
- `alarm-cutout-v2.png` - transparent animated clock
- `sneaker-cutout-v2.png` - transparent animated sneaker
- `keys-cutout-v2.png` - transparent animated keyring

Art direction: premium youth editorial photography, torn-paper collage, hard flash, screenprint grain, and a cobalt/red/yellow/ink palette. Each asset is used once.
