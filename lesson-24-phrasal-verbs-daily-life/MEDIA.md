# Media

The lesson uses two local WAV tracks in `assets/audio/`.

- `maya-morning.wav` - a daily-routine voice note used for gist and sequencing
- `action-shadow.wav` - a paced phrasal-verb shadowing track before the final performance

Both audio rails include play/pause controls and teacher-controlled transcript reveals. Audio controls stay in the document flow on phones so they cannot cover lesson text or navigation.
