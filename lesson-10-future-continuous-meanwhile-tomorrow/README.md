# Lesson 10 - Meanwhile, Tomorrow

An offline, teacher-led Pre-Intermediate 2 lesson for learners aged 13-17.

- Topic: Travel Plans
- Grammar: Future Continuous
- Creative identity: a kinetic world map and future-time lenses
- Scene count: 27
- Main file: `index.html`

## Teaching Route

The lesson moves from a personal future snapshot through travel vocabulary, grammar discovery, meaning, form, time expressions, pronunciation, controlled practice, a teacher-read listening, comprehension, pair information gap, timed group production, review, homework and an exit ticket.

## Controls

- `Right Arrow`, `Page Down`, or `Space`: next scene
- `Left Arrow` or `Page Up`: previous scene
- `M`: scene map
- `N`: teacher notes
- `F` button: fullscreen
- Swipe left or right on touch devices

All runtime assets are local. No network connection is required.
