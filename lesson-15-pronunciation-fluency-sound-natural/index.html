Weak forms · light grammar words

Not every word needs a microphone.

10
LIGHT
I can help you. → “can” is light; HELP is strong.
I want to go. → “to” is light; WANT / GO are strong.
Tea and cake. → “and” is light; TEA / CAKE are strong.
Weak forms · 5 min: Students do not need phonetic symbols here. Contrast a natural sentence with every word stressed equally.
Intonation · pitch makes meaning

Let your voice draw arrows.

11
RISE

Yes / no question ↗

Are you ready? Your voice often rises.

Wh- question ↘

Where are you going? Your voice often falls.

Tap a question. Choose ↗ for yes/no and ↘ for Wh- questions aloud.
Intonation · 7 min: Draw arrows in the air while learners repeat. Explain that real voices vary; this is a useful starting pattern.

Homework · practise the skill

19
HOME

Required

Write a 6-line dialogue. Underline key words, circle one linked chunk, and draw ↗ or ↘ on two questions. Practise it twice.

Optional challenge

Record your dialogue (30–45 seconds). Listen again and make one improvement to rhythm, linking, or intonation.

Checklist: clear key words · one link · one rising question · one falling question.
Homework · 3 min: Learners may record privately or only rehearse; the purpose is reflection and practice, not public performance.
