# Lesson 27 - Presence

Interactive Pre-Intermediate 2 lesson on presentation skills and speaking confidently for students aged 13-17.

## Creative direction

`Presence: Backstage to Spotlight` uses a cinematic rehearsal world rather than a conventional classroom template. Full-bleed film stills, editorial typography, stage-light motion, open compositions, and five scene transitions carry students through four acts:

1. Settle - posture, breath, and the first line.
2. Deliver - pace, pauses, emphasis, eye line, and gestures.
3. Shape - openings, points, examples, signposts, and closings.
4. Connect - visuals, online setup, questions, listening, rehearsal, and a final talk.

## Lesson format

- 28 teacher-led scenes with notes.
- Eight original lesson-specific images.
- Three local audio tracks for opening, pace, and model-talk practice.
- Seven-student roles and prompts throughout the lesson.
- Desktop, phone portrait, and phone landscape layouts.
- Vertical scrolling and browser pinch-zoom remain available on mobile.
- Horizontal swipe navigation is disabled while zooming, scrolling, using controls, or touching with more than one finger.

Open `index.html` directly or through the shared presentation viewer.
