# Teacher source notes

Reviewed 12 August 2026. These sources support the short factual statements in
the Myth Auction and the emergency-language examples. They are teacher notes,
not a substitute for local medical advice or emergency guidance.

- CDC, "Sleep and Health": teenagers ages 13-18 are recommended to get 8-10
  hours of sleep per 24 hours.
  https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-education/staying-healthy/sleep.html
- CDC, "Healthy Habits: Antibiotic Do's and Don'ts": antibiotics treat certain
  bacterial infections and do not work on viruses.
  https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/about/
- NHS, "Shortness of breath": severe difficulty breathing requires immediate
  emergency help.
  https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/
- NHS, "Chest pain": sudden chest pain that does not go away can require
  immediate emergency help.
  https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/chest-pain/
- MedlinePlus, "Teen Health": a broad, age-appropriate reference hub for teen
  physical and mental health topics.
  https://medlineplus.gov/teenhealth.html

The classroom cases are fictional and deliberately avoid diagnoses. Students
practise describing symptoms, asking follow-up questions, responding with
empathy, and choosing a sensible next step. In a real emergency, follow the
local emergency number and guidance for the country where the lesson is used.
