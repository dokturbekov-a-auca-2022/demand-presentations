# Lesson 03 — Frequency 103: The Body Broadcast

Interactive 23-scene HTML speaking lesson for **Medicine & Health** (Pre-Intermediate 2, ages 13–17), designed as a vivid analogue radio call-in show.

Open `index.html` in a current browser. The lesson has no external runtime dependencies and works offline when the complete folder is kept together.

## Controls

- Arrow keys, Page Up/Page Down, Space, or swipe: change scene
- `M`: scene directory
- `N`: teacher notes
- `F`: fullscreen or iPhone display instructions

## Lesson focus

Communication-first practice: vocabulary relay, symptom-remix improvisation,
body-location information gap, host fishbowl, live caller queue, defended
fact-checks, paired broadcast booths, consensus newsroom decisions, random
consultations, forbidden-word fluency, spoken repair, production, peer playback,
reflection, and homework.

The fictional cases are designed for language practice, not diagnosis or medical advice. Students may invent details and opt out of personal disclosure.

The exact prompts for the two original illustrations are documented in `ARTWORK.md`.

Health and safety statements used in the lesson were checked against official
public-health guidance. See `SOURCES.md` for the teacher-facing source list.
