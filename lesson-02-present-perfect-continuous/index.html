The apprentice who never stops

Alex is sixteen and helps at Ms Ortiz’s clock workshop after school. Alex has been working there for six weeks. On weekdays, Alex arrives at 4:30 and cleans the worktables before customers arrive.

This week is unusually busy. The town clock stopped on Monday, so Ms Ortiz and Alex have been repairing it every evening. Alex has also been delivering small parts by bicycle because the shop assistant is ill.

At home, things are busy too. Alex’s parents are working late, so Alex has been cooking dinner and taking care of a younger cousin. There is one problem: Alex hasn’t been sleeping enough and has been falling behind with homework.

Today, Ms Ortiz noticed Alex’s tired face. “You’re responsible,” she said, “but responsible people also ask for help.” They changed the schedule. Alex will deliver parts only twice a week, and another apprentice will share the cleaning.

While reading: Underline responsibilities. Circle time expressions. Put ★ beside the solution.
