# Original artwork prompts

## `assets/clockwork-town.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 hero background for an interactive English lesson
Primary request: a wildly imaginative handmade pop-up paper theatre called the Guild of Unfinished Things, showing a whimsical clockwork town at twilight where many everyday responsibilities are visibly still in progress
Scene/backdrop: layered paper city with crooked workshops, laundry lines, a bicycle courier, a cook preparing food, a student studying, a cleaner sweeping, a gardener watering plants, and an enormous brass clock tower whose gears trail through the streets
Subject: the entire town feels active and unfinished, with winding paths that lead toward the clock tower
Style/medium: tactile cut-paper collage, ink doodles, painted cardboard, visible fibers, torn edges, miniature theatre scenery; expressive and sophisticated enough for ages 13–17, not childish, not sleek, not corporate
Composition/framing: cinematic 16:9 wide establishing view with strong depth from foreground theatre curtains to distant clock tower; clear darker margins so light HTML text can overlay without covering important characters
Lighting/mood: theatrical amber footlights, indigo twilight, mysterious but warm, energetic and humorous
Color palette: ink navy, parchment cream, rusty orange, teal, brass gold, cherry red accents
Materials/textures: paper fibers, cardboard edges, ink hatching, small brass fasteners, handmade imperfections
Constraints: no written words, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no photorealism, no futuristic glossy UI
```

Composition/framing: cinematic 16:9 wide layout, four visually distinct but connected vignettes with clear actions and generous space between them
Lighting/mood: warm theatrical lighting, humorous visual details, energetic and humane
