# Lesson 25 - ORBIT 09: Signal Lost

An offline-first, teacher-led Pre-Intermediate 2 problem-solving lesson for students aged 13-17.

The 28-scene experience is structured as a playable orbital-station survival mission. Exactly seven crew roles remain active throughout the lesson. Students separate evidence from assumptions, choose a limited loadout, order emergency actions, classify priorities, repair an oxygen leak, compare routes, listen to two mission recordings, solve a visual search and access code, and deliver a 90-second launch briefing.

Three major decisions persist across the deck. The loadout, leak response, and route feed a crew score and produce a clean escape, delayed escape, or paused mission outcome.

Visual identity: grounded cinematic science fiction, moving scan lines, oxygen diagnostics, radar sweeps, emergency objects, star motion, and five scene transitions inspired by airlocks, glitches, hyperspace, diagnostics, and radar.

Controls: arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Space, stable horizontal swipe, `M` for the mission path, and `N` for teacher notes. Slides scroll vertically and support pinch zoom on phones.
