# Lesson 14 — Invented Here

Interactive 27-scene grammar lesson for **Passive Voice | Technology & Inventions** (Pre-Intermediate 2, ages 13–17).

Open `index.html` in a current browser. It works offline and includes keyboard/swipe navigation, a scene map (`M`), teacher notes (`N`), clickable vocabulary and grammar reveals, active/passive comparison, present/past/future/modal forms, picture-led process work, controlled practice, a teacher-read invention story, museum-label speaking, a timed invention pitch, review and exit ticket.

The visual identity is **Invented Here**: a midnight-blue maker lab with cyan, lime, orange and magenta paper-collage visuals. The lesson deliberately uses more visual figures than a text-only grammar deck: an invention hero, a four-invention card sheet, a four-step process figure, emoji object cues, timelines and process cards.

Original artwork and exact prompts are recorded in `ARTWORK.md`.
