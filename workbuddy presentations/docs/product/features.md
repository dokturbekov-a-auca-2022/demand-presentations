# PPT Outline

## Overview
A 20-slide, teacher-led online English lesson for high school students at Pre-Intermediate 2 level. The lesson revises core tenses and structures through seasonal updates, personal conversation, controlled practice, reading, breakout-room speaking, and a communicative interview task.

## Outline Content
1. Welcome Back — title and learning focus
2. Check-in Circle — greeting and icebreaker
3. Summer or Winter? — vocabulary introduction
4. Sort, Match, Speak — vocabulary practice
5. Today’s Mission — lesson objectives
6. The Time Machine — tense overview
7. Habit or Now? — present simple vs present continuous
8. Finished Past — past simple
9. Predictions and Decisions — future simple
10. Life Experience — present perfect
11. Describe the Scene — there is/there are
12. Compare It! — comparatives
13. Real Future Results — first conditional
14. Grammar Gym — controlled mixed practice
15. Two Seasonal Updates — reading and comprehension
16. Breakout Room Challenge — speaking practice
17. The Update Interview — production task
18. Grammar Quest — review game
19. Your Language Pack — homework
20. Exit Ticket — reflection and next lesson

## Design Style
Bright educational style for online teaching: warm white background, navy text, teal primary accents, coral and yellow activity accents, rounded but restrained panels, large readable type, and clear teacher/student task labels.
