# Original artwork prompts

All three assets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool,
then resized/compressed locally as WebP files for the presentation. No text is
baked into the images; labels and activities remain accessible HTML.

## `assets/hero-signal-noise.webp`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 hero and warm-up illustration for an interactive English lesson presentation
Primary request: Create a balanced visual metaphor for the question “Social media: good or bad?” without placing any words in the image.
Scene/backdrop: an imaginative editorial collage where a huge smartphone-shaped window divides and connects two simultaneous worlds
Subject: diverse teenagers aged 14–17; on one side they collaborate, share art, learn a skill, and maintain a long-distance friendship; on the other side one teen is distracted by endless scrolling, compares themself with polished images, and ignores a friend sitting nearby. Keep all situations ordinary, safe, and believable.
Style/medium: premium contemporary risograph editorial illustration, screen-printed ink layers, halftone grain, torn-paper edges, hand-cut geometric shapes, expressive but age-appropriate
Composition/framing: cinematic landscape, strong central phone-window silhouette, six clear discoverable details, balanced visual weight, room near the upper left and lower right for HTML title overlays
Lighting/mood: energetic, curious, ambiguous rather than frightening
Color palette: electric cobalt blue, coral red, acid yellow, off-white paper, touches of black; no clay, no stop-motion, no radio equipment, no steampunk
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no recognizable social-media logos, no watermarks, no distorted hands, no duplicate people, no unsafe content
```

## `assets/maya-week.webp`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 reading-comprehension illustration for an interactive English lesson
Primary request: Illustrate a teenager carrying out a one-week experiment to improve how she uses social media, with no written words in the image.
Scene/backdrop: a cozy bedroom-study space shown as one coherent editorial scene with subtle diary-like time progression from morning to evening
Subject: one 16-year-old girl using a phone intentionally: turning off attention-grabbing alerts, placing the phone face-down while studying, video-calling a distant cousin, photographing her artwork to share, and later taking a walk with a friend. Use symbolic notification shapes but no numbers, letters, logos, or readable screens.
Style/medium: sophisticated contemporary risograph editorial collage, screen-printed ink, halftone grain, torn paper, slightly imperfect registration, matching a premium youth magazine
Composition/framing: landscape; protagonist remains recognizable across small vignette fragments; clear left-to-right reading path; useful quiet margin for HTML labels
Lighting/mood: thoughtful, warm, hopeful, realistic rather than moralizing
Color palette: electric cobalt, coral, acid yellow, off-white, charcoal, a small amount of mint
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no brand logos, no watermark, no clay or stop-motion, no radio imagery, no duplicated limbs, no distorted hands
```

## `assets/four-profiles.webp`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 discussion and information-gap illustration for a teenage English lesson
Primary request: Create four distinct social-media user portraits that invite students to infer different experiences, with no words in the artwork.
Scene/backdrop: one bold editorial spread divided into four irregular torn-paper zones
Subject: four diverse teenagers aged 14–17, one per zone: a young musician happily sharing a homemade song with a small online audience; a football player distracted by phone alerts during homework; a quiet new student finding a welcoming hobby group online; a confident-looking creator taking many versions of one selfie and then looking tired. Show context through props and body language, not text.
Style/medium: contemporary risograph magazine illustration, visible halftone dots, screen-print ink, collage texture, dynamic geometric cropping
Composition/framing: landscape four-character ensemble, each zone clearly readable from classroom distance, expressive faces and hands, no visual zone presented as purely heroic or villainous
Lighting/mood: lively, humane, thought-provoking
Color palette: cobalt blue, coral, acid yellow, mint, off-white and charcoal
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, readable screens, logos, watermarks, clay, stop-motion, radio studio, steampunk, distorted hands, duplicated limbs
```
