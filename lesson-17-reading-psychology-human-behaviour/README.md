# Lesson 17 — Mind Atlas

Interactive 27-scene reading lesson for **Psychology & Human Behaviour** (Pre-Intermediate 2, ages 13–17).

This deck intentionally uses a different interaction language from the earlier presentations: field-note pages, evidence pins, fold-out vocabulary, lens toggles, text annotation marks, a spotlight meter, sequence strips, case files, perspective swaps, and a gallery-walk response trail. It is not a diagnosis lesson; students work with fictional or public examples and practise separating observation from interpretation.

Open `index.html` in a current browser. It works offline with keyboard/swipe navigation, the Mind Atlas route map (`M`), teacher notes (`N`), two short readings, comprehension, discussion and production work.

Original artwork and exact prompts are recorded in `ARTWORK.md`.
