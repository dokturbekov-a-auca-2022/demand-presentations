06 · MEANING

A QUESTION TAG IS A
MINI-QUESTION AFTER A STATEMENT.

CHECK A FACT

“The meeting starts at four, doesn't it?”

I think this is true, but I want confirmation.
INVITE AGREEMENT

“That song is catchy, isn't it?”

I expect you may agree with my opinion.
SOFTEN AN OPENER

“It's quieter here, isn't it?”

This is warmer than asking a sudden direct question.

iFor each example, say what the speaker wants: information, agreement, or a friendly start.

23 · MODEL BEFORE YOU SPEAK

A GOOD OPENER
DOES NOT STOP
AT THE TAG.

1 · NOTICEThe school festival starts today.

2 · TAGIt looks busy, doesn't it?

3 · ANSWERYes, especially near the music stage.

4 · DETAILMy friend's band is playing there.

5 · FOLLOW UPReally? What kind of music do they play?

6 · RETURNMostly rock. How about you?

7 · CONTINUEI like pop, but I want to hear the bands.
